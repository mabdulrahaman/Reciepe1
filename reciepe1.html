Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

These classic chocolate chip cookies are soft, sweet, and delicious. They are perfect for dessert or an evening snack.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup chocolate chips
  • 1/2 cup butter

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix butter and sugar in a bowl.
  3. Add flour and chocolate chips, then mix well.
  4. Place small cookie dough balls on a baking tray.
  5. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes.
